Market Segments
Every buyer segment has an envelope your product has to fit inside — a power ceiling their supply can feed, a thermal ceiling their cooling can carry, and a compatibility floor below which the part just causes problems. Miss the envelope and that segment simply can't buy it. These ceilings drift upward as the industry matures.

An approach

Change Price

Cutting price widens demand and thins margin. Raising it does the opposite — and buyers notice mid-life price hikes.

Ship a Driver Update

Push new low-level software to hardware already in the field. Newer silicon takes the new work cleanly; older models take what they can get.

Direct R&D

Point this month's research budget at one thing. Product areas raise what your parts can do; proprietary processes raise the ceilings everyone else is still stuck under. Gains are randomized but scale with spend.
